<CaseText id="problem" className="pt-32" sectionHeading="Overview" title="What is Social Prescribing?">
**Social prescribing** is a structured way to route people from a clinical encounter to non-clinical community supports such as 
housing, fitness, food security, and social connection. 

A **Link Worker** is the human liason connecting people to the community supports, often working in a SALC.

A **SALC** is a community centre that provides social prescribing services.

**OACAO** is the provincial body that funds the centres and collects their year-end data.

</CaseText>

<CaseText sectionHeading="The Problem" title="Every SALC did their year-end report by hand.">
Every year, reporting to OACAO is a paper chase that takes days. Link Workers have to cross-reference paper binders, Excel sheets, and SurveyMonkey forms to reconstruct a year of work.

Though time consuming, the report is a requirement for OACAO to fund the centres.
</CaseText>

<CaseText sectionHeading="The Challenge">
How do we reduce the time spent on the report, **while making OACAO's funding case easier to prove at the same time?**
</CaseText>

<CaseGroup id="discovery" variant="discovery">
<CaseText sectionHeading="Discovery" title="Every centre tracks differently.">
Interviewing 10 SALCs, each described a different way of managing clients, but OACAO's year-end report asked for data they didn't keep in one place, or didn't keep at all.

> "I don't know if you know all about the reporting process, but it's, it's like a three-day event to try and do the final report."

</CaseText>

<CaseText sectionHeading="Constraint" title="The backstage was a privacy problem.">
We were handling personal health information, which in Ontario means two laws: **PHIPA** (the Personal Health Information Protection Act) and **PIPEDA** (Canada's federal privacy law). 

This meant that centres keep their clients' identities while OACAO only ever sees numbers.
</CaseText>
</CaseGroup>

<CaseGroup id="decision" variant="discovery">
<CaseFixed sectionHeading="The Key Decision" title="Choosing which part of the service to improve">
We approached our solution by looking at the service from end-to-end, with all the frontstage and backstage dependancies in mind.

Having this wholistic view gave us a better understanding of where Link Workers were hurting the most, and where a digital solution would be most impactful.

For Link Workers, LinkLog helped the most when it **standardized their follow up and reporting workflows**. 

For OACAO, it was most rewarding **to be on the same platform as Link Workers**, and to **get all reports from every SALC in one place**.

<CaseGroup id="implementation" variant="discovery">
<CaseFixed sectionHeading="The Build" title="Building the platform over the weekend">

We used a lot of AI, but in targeted and meaningful ways. It was important to **validate its output across models**, but what was more crucial to our success was **communicating our intent through carefully crafted prompts**.

We shipped a working platform instead of a Figma prototype to showcase how our solution made real impact, doing so through scenario walkthroughs as Brenda and the OACAO.
